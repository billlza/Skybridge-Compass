\section{Scope}
This supplement is intentionally subordinate to the main manuscript. It will
hold detailed encodings, derivations of theorems stated in the main text,
extended evidence tables, and reproduction instructions. It will not introduce
an unreferenced theorem, a new protocol, or a separate contribution track.

\section{Formal Artifact Inventory}
For every main-text property, the final supplement will list the exact theorem or
lemma name, model revision, tool and version, assumptions, compromise escapes,
executability lemma, negative control, report digest, and the main-text claim it
supports. EasyCrypt, Tamarin, ProVerif, and the state model will remain separate
rows because their abstractions and conclusions differ.

\section{Extended Evaluation Matrix}
The final matrix will identify experimental unit, endpoint and binary identity,
transport, purpose, suite, policy, attack or ablation, batch structure, primary
metric, confidence interval, raw-data path, and generator for each reported
result. Static checks, simulator runs, physical devices, bare-metal hosts, and
published artifacts will be labeled as different evidence classes.

\section{Reproduction}
The anonymous review artifact will provide one entry point that validates the
claim ledger and manifest, rebuilds formal reports, compiles the normative core
and adapters in isolated checkouts, runs the bounded portable tests, and
regenerates every submitted table and figure from raw inputs. Hardware-dependent
lanes will have separate commands and will not be reported as reproduced when
only their historical transcripts are present.

\section{Open Evidence Items}
At this draft stage, the integrated codec, formal theories, state model,
bilateral-ready implementation, trusted effect committers, cross-language
corpus, final binaries, physical-device sessions, non-Apple interoperability,
application receipts, raw performance data, and immutable
evidence manifest are all pending. This section will be removed only as each item
is replaced by source-bound evidence and the claim ledger validates the state
transition.
